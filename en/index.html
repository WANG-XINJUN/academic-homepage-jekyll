About Me

I am an Associate Professor at School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Shandong Normal University. My research interests include nonlinear systems, multi-agent systems, and cyber-physical systems, with a particular focus on secure and resilient control, event-triggered control, state estimation, and adaptive control. My research aims to develop efficient control and estimation strategies for networked multi-agent systems under communication constraints, cyber attacks, and partial state information, with applications to cooperative robotics and networked control systems.
